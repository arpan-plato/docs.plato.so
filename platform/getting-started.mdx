Plato is where you build a realistic slice of real work — the apps, the files, the data — and set an AI agent loose on a task inside it, then let a grader check whether the AI got it right. This guide is about **using the platform itself**: the screens, the buttons, and the flow from a blank world to a submitted task.

## Sign in

We'll send you a **special access link** that signs you in as a **creator**. Open that link, complete sign-in, and you'll land on the **Worlds** dashboard — your home base.

<Note>
Use the link we sent you — that's what grants your creator access. If you sign in without it and don't see the creator tools, reach out and we'll get you set up.
</Note>

## The Worlds dashboard

Everything starts from a **world**: a workspace where you combine **simulators** (the apps, like Gmail or Google Sheets) and **datasets**, and build a task on top of them. Each world holds **exactly one task**.

The dashboard lists your worlds as cards. Each card shows the world's **name**, its **status** (for example *Running* or *Stopped*), its size and creation date, and a row of quick actions on the right: **tag**, **clone**, and **archive**.

### Finding your worlds

Your **running** worlds are listed first (under **Active**); **stopped** ones follow (under **Idle**). As your list grows, two controls help you navigate:

- **Filter by name**, or by **tag**.
- **Show archived** to reveal worlds you've archived out of the list.

Each world also carries a **review status** as you build and submit it — *In progress*, *In review*, *Approved*, or *Rejected*. More on that in [Submitting for review](/platform/submitting-and-review).

## Create a world

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name, describe, and tag it">
    Give the world a short, clear **name**, a brief **description** of the task you'll build, and a **tag**. Use one clear name per world — you'll build a single task inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Hit **+ Create**. The world spins up and opens in the editor, ready to build.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Duplicate check.** When you create a world, Plato checks your **name and description** against tasks that already exist. If it looks like a task someone has already built, you'll see it **flagged** — so two people don't build the same benchmark. If you're flagged, pick a different workflow or a different angle on it.
</Note>

<Note>
**One task per world.** Every world holds a single task — there's no way to add more, and nothing to manage. If you want to build another task on the **same seed data**, **clone** the world and change the task in the copy (see [Reusing seed data for a new task](/platform/building-a-world-and-task#reusing-seed-data-for-a-new-task)).
</Note>
