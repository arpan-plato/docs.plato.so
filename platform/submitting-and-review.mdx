When a task is ready, you submit it to us to review. This page covers that hand-off and what happens after.

## Submit for review

Click **Submit for review** in the world header. Add a note with anything we should know — what the task covers, edge cases you handled, or anything you're unsure about.

## What happens next

<Steps>
  <Step title="The world freezes">
    Submitting stops the workspace and makes the world **read-only**, with an **In review** badge. It now shows up under the **In review** tab on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Need to keep working?">
    **Withdraw** it and the world unlocks again so you can edit. Resubmit when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We review it">
    We look it over and either **approve** or **reject** it. The outcome shows on your dashboard under the **Approved** or **Rejected** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track it and talk to us

- **Status at a glance:** the dashboard tabs — **In progress · In review · Rejected · Approved** — always show where each world stands.
- **Questions on a specific task:** open the **review timeline** (the comment icon in the world header) to leave a comment. We'll see it and reply there.

## If we send it back

You'll see the reason in the review timeline. Fix the task, and because every checkpoint saves a new **version**, your fixes are captured automatically — then **Resubmit**.

<Note>
The same version can't be resubmitted, so make sure you've made a change (which saves a new version) before resubmitting.
</Note>

## If we approve it

An approved world is **frozen** as the accepted deliverable — it can't be edited or resubmitted. To build on it, **Clone** it: that gives you a fresh, editable copy you can start from right away.

<Tip>
Clone (and **Take over**, for worlds you don't own) is available immediately — you never have to wait on us to keep building. A good approved task is often the starting point for the next one.
</Tip>
